# Specialist catalogue review

Generated by `scripts/specialist-catalogue-review.mjs`. Do not edit by hand.

The 266 Specialist definitions were drafted with model assistance and checked by validators and tests. **None of it has been reviewed by a property-management professional or by counsel.** It is operational guidance and product definition, never legal policy.

## What each field is

- **Routing metadata**, which only decides where work goes: `name`, `triggers`, `notThis`, `collaborators`. A mistake here causes a misroute, never an unauthorized action.
- **Capability definitions**, where code enforces the product's behavior (tools, checks), not the text: `capabilities`, `inputs`, `outputs`, `completion`, `execution`.
- **Operational guidance**, which briefs a model and is enforced by nothing: `boundary`.
- **Legal / compliance claims**, which must be reviewed and never relied on as policy: `forbidden`, `approvals`, plus any text below flagged as regulated.

**Where authority lives.** What may actually happen is decided by the deterministic layer, not by this catalogue:

- permissions (`lib/agents/permissions.ts`, `lib/agents/organization`)
- approval and financial policy (`lib/agents/policy.ts`, `execution-policy.ts`)
- the PMS capability matrix and mandatory human checkpoints (`lib/pms`)
- the workspace's operating profile

Every Specialist and Lead is briefed with that statement.
